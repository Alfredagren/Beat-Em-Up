## What Went Well?
Two or three things. What would you do the same way next time?

I liked working with the animator to create game mechanics. I will probably use that in my next project too
I'm happy with the feel of the movement and attacking. I will try and keep that feeling in the next game

## What Went Wrong?
Two or three things. Not just "I ran out of time": what caused it?

Spending hours fixating to fix one mechanic, like trying to make the enemies stop when attacking like the player, but however I tried just made it buggier and in the end I gave up
Not spending enough time thinking of a real level. Most of the game just comes down to randomly placed prefabs that follows the player

## What Would You Do Differently Next Time?
One or two changes you'll take into your next Game Project.

I would take advantage of the early stages of development more to try and add more features. I felt like I didn't cut enough so the gameplay feels a a little one-note
I want to spend more time in concept phase and trying to think of an interesting gameplay core loop so I don't just keep adding features on a flat game
